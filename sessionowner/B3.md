the existing backend, snapshot, guard and CAS. `SettlementAuthorization` is nil
by default and denies settlement. No wire selector, automatic invocation, new
service, real host enrollment or business continuation is introduced.

## Separate bounded control, never recovery as cleanup

Control has a fixed two-second owner budget and 250ms queue sub-bound, intersected
with the current caller/channel and strict non-detaching storage context. It does
not borrow an expired original business window. It rejects an active in-process
original issuer and cannot acquire over a held native guard. It calls no adapter,
provider, proof, delta, readiness, performInvocation, recover, publishRecovery,
acquisition, adoption or retry. Capability and backend metadata calls are the only
control callbacks. No control schedules later work.

## Original graph and terminal identity

The control follows the exact InitialID/RecoveryID/RetryID journals, original
intent/result/FinishID, claim adoption/prior IDs and related candidate-transition
IDs. Unknown, missing or contradictory readback remains fenced. A later result
or adoption noncommit cannot erase an earlier issued permit. A claimless issued
read with no committed completion has no current owner reconciliation API: the
block explicitly names unsupported safe-read outcome reconciliation, requiring a
separately admitted authoritative outcome route rather than forced clearing.
Existing exact-claim Recover is named only as a separately authorized route for
claim/candidate dependencies; settlement does not invoke it.

A positively committed original FinishID returns a metadata-only `historical`
status with the original correlation/certainty, never rewrites that outcome.
Otherwise, eligible facts produce purpose-separated deterministic preparation and
resolution IDs from immutable original root identity. Preparation stores an
irreversible no-continuation barrier, proposed disposition, original transaction
facts and exact journal/claim seals. Unknown preparation forbids resolution.
Lost acknowledgments and later bookkeeping failures cannot generate competing IDs.
Only exact positive resolution readback returns `settled`.

Original FinishID/payload, child histories, intents, effect IDs, reservations,

## Fences, status and future operations

A per-load, nonserialized verified-readback mark permits only that settled
composition and its covered old bookkeeping to stop contributing an execution
fence. Stored resolved markers alone are insufficient: ordinary later admission
reconciles preparation and resolution again. Independent claims, candidate
uncertainty, auth/maintenance/cooldown stops remain. Inspect performs read-only
verification; no implicit settlement occurs. Old composed permits refuse any
stored barrier, and old request lookup returns a failed/canceled terminal status
with the distinct resolution receipt after positive readback, never resumes work.

An active exact claim is preserved. A fresh normal business request or exact-claim
Recover is a distinct call with normal authorization, generation, budget and
fences. No-effect old ownership may settle while the claim still blocks ordinary
work; an authorized fresh exact-claim recovery can reconcile the retained old
intent and proceed without replaying the old Perform. Terminal authorization does
not authorize that follow-up. Historical cache truth is not rewritten; pending
projections have no business response to promote. An already canceled control does
no lookup and returns no invented terminal status; use a fresh authorized control
for actual metadata status.
